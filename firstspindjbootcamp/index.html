More Than DJ Lessons

Press Play on Possibility

Learning to DJ is about more than pressing play. At First Spin, students learn how to listen, prepare, create, communicate, perform and lead.

Our hands-on summer program introduces young people to professional DJ skills while building confidence, discipline, creativity and entrepreneurial thinking. No prior DJ experience is required — and because access matters, First Spin is offered to participating families at no cost.

About First Spin

Access, Creativity & Confidence

First Spin was created from a simple belief: young people deserve access — to equipment, mentorship, creative spaces, and professionals who can show them what's possible. Founder DJ ReRe knows what it feels like to learn without a roadmap, and built First Spin to create one for the next generation.

Our Mission. To empower youth and beginners through no-cost DJ education that merges creativity, entrepreneurship and confidence.

Our Vision. A future where diverse DJs help lead the sound of tomorrow — equipped with technical skill, business knowledge, confidence and opportunity.

“Music connects people. Learning to DJ can also teach you how to lead.”— DJ ReRe, Founder

What Students Learn

Seven Weeks. Real Skills. One Big Showcase.

A clear, beginner-friendly path that meets students where they are and builds toward a real live performance.

1
Week 1

Gear Setup & Sound Basics

How DJ equipment works, how to connect a basic setup, and how sound moves through a DJ system.

2
Weeks 2–3

Mixing, Beatmatching & Flow

Transitioning between songs, matching rhythms and creating a smooth musical journey.

3
Week 4

Music Theory & Playlist Building

Tempo, song structure, musical organization and how DJs build strong playlists and libraries.

4
Week 5

Branding & Entrepreneurship

Developing a DJ identity plus intro lessons in professionalism, branding and gig readiness.

5
Week 6

Stage Confidence & Crowd Control

How to present yourself, communicate through music and get comfortable performing.

6
Week 7

Live Showcase Performance

Students put their skills into action in front of family, friends and the community.

Our Why

A First Spin Can Change Everything

Young people need more than activities. They need places where they can explore who they are, build confidence, learn practical skills and feel heard.

First Spin was created to provide access to DJ education, professional mentorship and creative opportunities that many young people would otherwise never experience. We teach DJing to spark purpose, build confidence and open doors.

Meet the Founder

Meet DJ ReRe

DJ · Founder · Mentor · Culture Connector

First Spin was founded by professional DJ and entrepreneur DJ ReRe. Her journey into DJing began without easy access to equipment, mentors or a clear roadmap. She learned by doing.

Today, she uses her industry experience to create the type of learning environment she wishes had existed when she started — helping young people develop DJ skills, confidence, professionalism, creativity and entrepreneurial thinking.

“Music connects people. Learning to DJ can also teach you how to lead.”— DJ ReRe, Founder
