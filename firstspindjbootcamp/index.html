Why First Spin Exists

More Than Music Lessons

Many talented young people have the interest and creativity to become DJs — but not the equipment, instruction, mentors, or opportunities to get started. First Spin gives them a safe, encouraging place to be creative, be heard, and build real skills.

A Safe Creative Space

An encouraging environment where students can experiment, express themselves, and grow.

Real DJ Education

Professional instruction on real equipment and technology — not a toy or a phone app.

Access to Equipment

Hands-on time with controllers, headphones, and software many students don't have at home.

Confidence & Leadership

Skills in focus, communication, and stage presence that reach far beyond the DJ booth.

Entrepreneurship

Early exposure to branding, gig readiness, and the business behind the music.

Mentorship & Community

Positive role models and a supportive crew of peers who lift each other up.

Meet the Founder

Meet DJ ReRe

DJ · Founder · Mentor · Culture Connector

DJ ReRe is an international DJ, entrepreneur, and mentor — and the founder of a South Florida female DJ entertainment and event-production company. She built First Spin to open doors she had to fight to walk through herself.

Her work connects DJ culture with leadership, confidence, performance, and entrepreneurship. As lead mentor, she brings professional experience, an expanding network across South Florida's music and event community, and a deep commitment to expanding access for the next generation of young creatives.

“Music is the connector to the world. When you learn to DJ, you learn to lead.” — DJ ReRe, Founder

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate?

First Spin welcomes youth and beginners ages 8 and older. Curiosity and a willingness to learn are all that's required.

Does my child need DJ experience?

No. The program is built for beginners and starts with the basics — most students have never touched DJ gear before.

Is the program really offered at no cost?

Yes. Participation is free for students. The program is sustained by donors, sponsors, and community partners.

Does my child need to own DJ equipment?

No. Students learn hands-on using equipment provided during the program.

Where are classes held?

What happens during the live showcase?

In Week 7, students perform a live DJ set — putting everything they've learned on stage in front of family and friends.

Can parents attend the showcase?

Absolutely — families are encouraged to come celebrate their student's progress.

How can a company sponsor the program?

Use the Sponsor & Partner form above. Cash sponsorships have the greatest impact, but we welcome many kinds of support.

What equipment donations are most useful?

DJ controllers, laptops, professional headphones, studio monitors, cables, and DJ software or music subscriptions. Share what you have via the Equipment form.

Can DJs volunteer or guest teach?

Yes. We welcome qualified mentors and guest instructors to supplement our professional team. Reach out through the Sponsor & Partner form.

How can a school or organization host First Spin?

We'd love to explore it. Start a partnership conversation and we'll design something that fits your community.
